
*Assignment: TED Talk Presentation*

Each student is required to select a relevant TED or TEDx Talk related to leadership, motivation, human behaviour, organizational performance, personal development, or a related subject.

Presentation Format

Slide 1: Title Slide

- Title of the TED Talk
- Name of the speaker
- Student name, registration number and section
- Course title

Slide 2: Introduction to the TED Talk

- Brief introduction to the speaker and their relevant background
- Context of the talk
- Video link and duration

Slide 3: Central Theme and Purpose

- What is the talk about?
- What key issue or problem does the speaker address?
- What is the central message?

Slide 4: Main Ideas and Key Messages

- Identify and explain 3–5 major ideas.
- Highlight significant examples, stories or evidence used by the speaker.

Slide 5: Leadership and Motivation Concepts

- Identify relevant leadership and/or motivation theories and concepts.
- Explain how these relate to the speaker's ideas.
- Examples may include transformational leadership, emotional intelligence, intrinsic motivation, Self-Determination Theory and positive psychology.

Slide 6: Critical Analysis

- Which arguments are convincing, and why?
- What evidence supports the speaker's claims?
- What are the limitations, assumptions or unanswered questions?
- Are the ideas applicable in all contexts?

Slide 7: Practical Applications

- How can the ideas be applied by students, managers, organizational leaders and teams?
- Provide at least two specific practical examples.

Slide 8: Personal Learning and Reflection

- What are the three most important lessons learned?
- How has the talk influenced your understanding of leadership and motivation?
- What specific actions could you take as a result?

Slide 9: Conclusion

- Summarize the central message.
- Explain the relevance of the talk to leadership and motivation.
- Present one key takeaway.

Slide 10: References

- TED Talk title, speaker, year and URL.
- References for theories, research articles, books and other sources used.
- Follow APA 7th edition referencing style.

Presentation Requirements

- Duration: 10–12 minutes, followed by 3–5 minutes of questions and discussion.
- Use approximately 8–10 content slides, excluding the title and references slides.
- Use concise bullet points rather than lengthy paragraphs.
- Show the video or an appropriate excerpt as instructed.
- Explain and analyse the content in your own words.
- Support theoretical interpretations and critical analysis with credible academic sources.
