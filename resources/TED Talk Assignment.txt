*Assignment submisdion: Critical Analysis of a TED Talk*

Each student is required to submit a written academic analysis of the same TED or TEDx Talk selected for the oral presentation.

Required Format

Cover Page

- University and department
- Course title
- Assignment title
- TED Talk title and speaker
- Student name and registration number
- Instructor's name
- Submission date

1. Introduction (150–200 words)

Introduce the topic of the TED Talk, explain its relevance to leadership and motivation, and state the purpose of your analysis.

2. Speaker Background and Context (100–150 words)

Briefly describe the speaker's relevant background, expertise and the context of the talk.

3. Summary of the TED Talk (250–350 words)

Present the central argument, main ideas, key messages and significant examples. Write in your own words rather than reproducing the video transcript.

4. Analysis of Leadership and Motivation Concepts (300–400 words)

Identify and explain at least two relevant theories, frameworks or concepts. Discuss how the speaker's arguments illustrate, support, extend or challenge these concepts. Support your analysis with academic literature.

5. Critical Evaluation (250–350 words)

Evaluate the strengths and limitations of the speaker's arguments. Consider the quality of evidence, underlying assumptions, alternative explanations and the contexts in which the ideas may or may not apply.

6. Practical Applications (200–300 words)

Explain how the ideas can be applied in academic settings, teams, workplaces or organizational leadership. Include at least two specific examples and discuss expected benefits and possible implementation challenges.

7. Personal Reflection and Learning (150–200 words)

Discuss the most important lessons learned, how the talk relates to your understanding of leadership and motivation, and what actions you might take in response.

8. Conclusion (100–150 words)

Summarize your analysis, the talk's relevance to leadership and motivation, and its principal implications.

9. References

List the TED Talk and all academic and other sources cited in the text, following APA 7th edition referencing style.

10. Appendix (Optional)

Include supplementary material only where useful.

Written Submission Requirements

- Length: 1,500–2,000 words, excluding the cover page and reference list.
- Academic sources: At least five credible academic sources, including relevant peer-reviewed research.
- Referencing: APA 7th edition.
- Format: Word document or PDF, not PowerPoint.
- Writing style: Formal academic prose with clear headings.
- Originality: All sources must be acknowledged, and the analysis must reflect your own understanding and critical thinking.

Important Academic Requirement

Students must distinguish between:

1. What the speaker actually claims.
2. What relevant academic theories and research suggest.
3. Their own critical interpretation and conclusions.

The written submission must not simply reproduce the PowerPoint slides. It should develop the ideas into a coherent academic analysis supported by relevant literature.
